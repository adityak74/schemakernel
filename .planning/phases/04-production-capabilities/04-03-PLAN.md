- User Session → Policy Resolver: Session is mapped to a policy version.

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |

| T-04-03-01 | Tampering | Policy Alias | mitigate | Ensure alias updates are restricted to authorized administrators (though auth is out of scope for this core engine, the design should support it). |
